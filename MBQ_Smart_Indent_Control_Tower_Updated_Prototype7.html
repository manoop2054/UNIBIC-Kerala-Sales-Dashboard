INDENT CBB is the CFA-item share of Sales Head IND. Net Requirement = INDENT CBB − Current SOH − Confirmed Incoming (minimum 0). Last 2M Avg CBB is a planning reference only; do not add it to INDENT. Sales Head enters any additional manual quantity in “Input Your CBB”.

Allocation decision steps

1 · Calculate CFA-specific demandLast 2 months billing average for that exact CFA + Item, not Kerala-wide average.
2 · Adjust for stock and confirmed supplyNet Requirement = INDENT CBB − Current SOH − Confirmed Incoming Supply (minimum 0). Last 2M Avg CBB is reference only.
3 · Allocate within plant availabilityRisk/shortage priority, stock cover and available CBB are considered; show any remaining shortage rather than hiding it.
