Recommended plant request is based on CFA-item Net Requirement. Uploaded Plant TO quantities are included as confirmed incoming supply; absent TO data is not treated as confirmed stock-out.

Allocation decision steps

1 · Calculate CFA-specific demandLast 2 months billing average for that exact CFA + Item, not Kerala-wide average.
2 · Adjust for stock and confirmed supplyNet Requirement = Avg CBB + Target Closing Stock − Usable SOH − Confirmed Incoming Supply. Target closing stock must be configured using approved business rules.
3 · Allocate within plant availabilityRisk/shortage priority, stock cover and available CBB are considered; show any remaining shortage rather than hiding it.

Billing window settings

Planning principleMonth-end stock requirement is calculated after deducting expected mid-month consumption and considering any confirmed receipts between windows. Peak targets are billing value targets; SKU-level stock quantities require item mix / rate data from the workbook.

Mid-month checklist

13th–15th

☐ Confirm plant dispatch cut-off using 4–5 day lead time.

☐ Check CFA-wise stock cover for priority SKU groups.

☐ Include only confirmed incoming PO before the window.

☐ Reserve stock against the approved peak SKU mix.

Month-end checklist

29th–31st

☐ Roll forward post-mid-month stock and actual billing.

☐ Deduct forecast consumption between windows.

☐ Re-check lead time and confirmed receipts.

☐ Prevent duplicate allocation of stock to both peaks.
